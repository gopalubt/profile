Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

What this site collects

This is a static portfolio site. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect or store personal information on a server. There is no contact form, no analytics script, and no advertising or tracking pixel.

Local storage

Your light/dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it on your next visit. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Third-party services

Stylesheets and fonts are loaded from the jsDelivr CDN, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Both providers may log your IP address and browser user agent as part of serving those requests. Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.
